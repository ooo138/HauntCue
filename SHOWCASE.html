Your show. Your timing.

Build the cue sheet

Drag prop cues on a timeline. Add servo movements and lighting event windows. Keep up to four scenes, with editable timings and JSON backups.

Put sound in the scene

Use up to two serial players: DFPlayer Mini, DY-SV8F or mixed. Keep ambient playing uninterrupted while the other module handles scare tracks. Load browser reference audio to see its waveform while arranging cues.

Make it move

Servo sweeps, trembles, peeks and quick jerks. Set angles, pauses and repeats—or record a slider performance and edit the resulting moves.

See the prop

Start with a stage preset or sketch with lines. Drop motors, lights and sensors; bind them to channels for scene preview and sampled live state. Click devices to edit parameters and test saved hardware.

Control it anywhere nearby

Use the mobile-friendly browser interface on your network or connect directly to its hotspot. Name controllers and discover others on the same network.

Set it up your way

Configurable pins, HIGH/LOW activation, device labels, trigger debounce and random repeat. Includes board wiring views, an installation package and setup wizard.

Light the atmosphere. Then change the mood.

Ambient glow → event effect

Each lighting channel has separate ambient and event profiles. A cue can turn a quiet storm into a toxic puke glow or a scare strobe, then return to ambient. Customize brightness, speed, colors and lightning timing.

Your workshop, ready to reuse.

A dedicated device inventory offers 20 hardware presets and custom profiles. Save whole props with their cue sheets, visual layout and device assignments. Export profiles and prop backups to share between controllers, and start a fresh controller with the setup wizard.

Choose your board.

One Windows installer detects classic ESP32, C3, S2, S3 or C6 and selects the matching build. C3 offers four switched outputs, two lights, a servo and one audio player. Other profiles offer six switched outputs. New families are experimental and await hardware testing; the classic WROOM build is bench-boot tested.
