The project uses the custom HauntCue Source-Available License 1.0, whose complete terms are in LICENSE. There is no standard SPDX license identifier for this custom text; do not label it MIT or GPL.

## What Rick selected

- Free use and private modifications are allowed, including using your own prop at a paid attraction.
- Free redistribution is allowed with notices, the same license and the corresponding source for the version shared.
- Selling HauntCue or a controller, kit or prop with it supplied requires Rick's explicit written permission beforehand. Merely notifying him is insufficient.
- Optional donations are allowed. Requiring a donation or purchase to obtain a copy counts as charging.

This is source-available, not OSI-approved open source. Both MIT and GNU GPL permit sales without the author's individual approval, so neither matches the selected commercial condition. The earlier unpublished MIT/GPL proposals were not selected. No MIT/GPL license for HauntCue is included in this release; third-party files may independently carry those licenses.

## Before publishing

This custom license is a reviewable project draft, not a legal opinion or a guarantee of enforceability. Have a qualified lawyer review it before relying on its commercial restrictions, particularly when distributing firmware linked with separately licensed SDK/library components. LICENSE contains the terms; this guide is a summary.

Publish the complete editable HauntCue source and build instructions alongside a binary release. Preserve all separately applicable dependency/tool notices. Permission from Rick covers rights he holds, and does not waive rights belonging to other contributors or third-party projects. Future third-party contributions may require additional permission for a separately licensed commercial offering.

The Windows esptool executable is a separate GPLv2 program: its own license and source-distribution rights remain intact. ArduinoJson uses MIT, and the Arduino core/SDK contain separately licensed code. The HauntCue sale condition does not relicense or restrict those independent components. See THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md.

Audio loaded onto a player can have separate rights. The source-upload package excludes audio tracks. The existing procedural audio-starter files carry their own permission in audio-starter/README.txt if distributed separately; keep that notice.
