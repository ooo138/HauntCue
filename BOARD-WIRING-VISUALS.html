Your photographed ESP32 MiniKit-style · FOUR rows · underside

Photo-matched pad labels. UNDERSIDE facing you, USB at bottom. LO/LI/RI/RO mean left outer / left inner / right inner / right outer; row 1 is the top pad. These are drawing coordinates, not connector numbers. TCK = GPIO13. Manufacturer/revision is unconfirmed. Do not mirror this underside view onto the component side.

LOLIN D32 v1.0 · connector reference

Schematic connector-order reference: JP1/JP2 pins 1–16, not an asserted physical top-view orientation. Use connector numbering and silkscreen. REG-EN is regulator enable; VBAT is battery power. GPIO35 is used for battery sensing.
